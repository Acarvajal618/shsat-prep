Mad-Lib Generator — Demo

Proof-of-concept for one skill: Vague Pronoun. Not a real exam question — every question is assembled on the spot from name/scenario pieces.

How this works: this page combines 6 name pairs × 5 short scenarios = 30 different paragraphs, each with a pronoun planted at a different spot. Get one right, and it hands you a new combination. It's not infinite — but it's enough that you won't see the same exact paragraph twice for a while.

    What would it take to make this real? This is a template randomizer, not AI — all 30 combinations are fixed in advance in the code, just shuffled and filled in on click. Making it a real "infinite" generator (actually novel sentences every time) would mean calling an AI model live from the page, which this static site can't safely do on its own — that needs a small backend to hold an API key and would cost money per generation. The template approach you're looking at here is the same technique your Equation/Percentage/Ratio trainers already use, just applied to sentences instead of numbers — more templates and name/scenario pools can be added any time to grow the variety.
